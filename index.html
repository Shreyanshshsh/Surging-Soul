Meet Swati Amrit

Swati Amrit is a certified Life Coach and Energy Healer with over two decades of experience in education, research, and human behaviour. She works with professionals who are intellectually capable and outwardly high-functioning yet find themselves stuck in cycles of burnout, overthinking, emotional reactivity, and chronic stress they cannot seem to think their way out of.

The reason, more often than not, is not a lack of skill or strategy. It is the nervous system running old survival responses in a professional context, responses shaped long before the career began. Swati's work addresses that root layer: the unresolved emotional experiences and deeply held subconscious beliefs that quietly drive behaviour, undermine confidence, and keep capable people operating well below their actual capacity. Her approach combines structured coaching with evidence-informed somatic and subconscious work, creating a space where lasting change becomes possible, not just intellectual insight.

Every engagement is designed to help youRecognise. Regulate. Rebuild.
